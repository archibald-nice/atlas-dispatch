Map geometry
Made with Natural Earth. 1:110m land vector data is in the public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Source and derivation details: assets/land-provenance.json.
The land particles are environmental texture, not news records or activity counts.
Administrative outlines use Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 Countries (public domain).
The pinned source, checksum and conversion method are in assets/country-boundaries.json.
The dataset follows Natural Earth's de facto boundary policy; display is not a statement of sovereignty.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/disputed-boundaries-policy/
